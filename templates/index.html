    How it works
    
        
    Three steps, all automatic. You only pick what to summarize and how long the summary should be.
    
        
      
          
    1. 
            
      Add your content
      
            
      Upload a PDF or image, paste a YouTube link, or add a video or audio file.
      
          
      2. 
          
    2. 
            
      SummaBrowse reads it
      
            
      Text is pulled from the file, scanned pages are read with OCR, and speech is transcribed.
      
          
      3. 
          
    3. 
            
      Get the key points
      
            
      Gemini writes a short, medium or detailed summary you can copy or download.
      
          
      4. 
        
    
      
    
    
    

    
    
      
    
        
    What it handles
    
        
    
          
    
            
    
            
    PDFs
    
            
    Reports, papers, contracts and notes, up to {{ max_upload_mb }} MB.
    
          
    
          
    
            
    
            
    Scanned pages and photos
    
            
    Text in images and scanned PDFs is read with OCR, up to 20 pages.
    
          
    
          
    
            
    
            
    YouTube videos
    
            
    Summarized from the video's captions, or by Gemini watching the video when there are none.
    
          
    
          
    
            
    
            
    Video and audio files
    
            
    Lectures, meetings and podcasts up to {{ max_video_minutes }} minutes are transcribed, then summarized.
    
          
    
        
    
      
    
    
    

    
    
      
    
        
    
          
    Your files stay yours
    
          
    SummaBrowse only keeps your file for as long as it takes to read it.
    
        
    
        
      
          
    • Uploads are deleted as soon as the summary is ready.
      • 
          
    • Summaries are never saved on the server. Downloads are created in your browser.
      • 
          
    • No account and no tracking.
      • 
          
    • Text is sent to Google Gemini to write the summary.
