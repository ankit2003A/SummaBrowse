How to Use This Summarizer

1. Upload Your File

Upload a PDF or image, or paste a YouTube link.

2. Process the File

Our AI processes your file and extracts the key points.

3. Adjust Summary

Pick a short, medium or detailed summary.

4. Download Summary

Get your summary in seconds and download it as a text file.

Why Use This Summarizer?

Free

No sign-up and no payment needed.

Accurate

Summaries come from Google Gemini, with an offline fallback if it is unavailable.

Secure

Uploaded files are deleted right after processing. Summaries are never stored on the server.

Fast

Most documents are summarized in a few seconds.

Flexible

Works with text PDFs, scanned PDFs, images, YouTube links and video or audio files.

About

SummaBrowse is built by Ankit Verma. It combines OCR (Tesseract), YouTube captions and Gemini to turn long documents and videos into clear summaries. The source code is on GitHub.
